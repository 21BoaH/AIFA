Project background & scope

Problem statement & objectives

Investigating how client-side edge computer vision can remove the barrier to injury-free resistance training, without specialised sensors.

The challenge

Poor form is invisible to the person lifting

Improper exercise kinematics in resistance training frequently lead to musculoskeletal injury and inefficient neuromuscular recruitment. Beginners often lack access to qualified supervision or immediate feedback on their form.

Edge computer vision

33 full-body 3D landmarks at 30+ frames per second. Inter-joint vectors are computed in real time to detect eccentric and concentric phases and evaluate depth and range of motion.

Privacy by architecture

Inference runs client-side on WebAssembly and WebGL. No raw video frames or biometric telemetry are ever sent to a remote server.

Technical implementation

System architecture & pipeline

A deterministic four-stage pipeline that turns raw webcam video into quantified repetition counts and posture cues.

Read the maths
  1. 01

    Video frame ingestion

    Captures the camera stream through the HTML5 WebRTC API and buffers live frames into a hidden video element at the device’s native refresh rate.

    getUserMedia()
  2. 02

    MediaPipe Pose inference

    A lightweight convolutional pose landmarker infers 33 key landmarks per frame: normalised X, Y, Z coordinates plus a visibility confidence score.

    pose.send(frame)
  3. 03

    Vector joint trigonometry

    Joint angles between adjacent segments (shoulder–elbow–wrist, hip–knee–ankle) come from the two-argument arctangent, mapped to degrees and smoothed.

    Math.atan2(y, x)
  4. 04

    Finite state machine

    Each rep must pass through every phase in order. A repetition is counted only once its anatomical thresholds have all been met.

    START → DOWN → UP
